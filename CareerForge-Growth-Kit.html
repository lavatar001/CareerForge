02 — Landing pages

Three directions to test

Each is a distinct angle, not just a different colour. Ship all three as variants, send traffic, keep the winner. Every Nano Banana prompt is written to render the exact headline text (Nano Banana handles in-image text well) — paste straight into Gemini image generation, 16:9 for the hero.

Frustrated applicants getting silence; ATS-anxious professionals

03 — Positioning

Ten marketing angles

Each pairs a one-line hook with the psychological lever it pulls and where it fits.

1

The 6-second rejection

"Rejected in 6 seconds by a robot you never met."

Lever: fear + injustice. The ~75% ATS-rejection stat weaponised. Highest-converting hook in the niche. → paid ads, TikTok.

2

Built for the Gulf

"Job tools are built for America. This one is built for the Gulf."

Lever: belonging. Your defensible moat. Owns a market incumbents ignore. → landing, LinkedIn, local press.

3

The lettre de motivation

"Une lettre de motivation qui ne sent pas la traduction Google."

Lever: cultural authenticity. English-first tools botch it; you don't. → francophone channels, Le CYCLE cross-promo.

4

The AED 500 alternative

"AED 500 for one CV rewrite? Or unlimited for AED 45 a month."

Lever: price anchoring. Undercut Dubai's human CV writers 10×. → search ads on "CV writing Dubai".

5

Outcome, not document

"Stop building resumes. Start getting interviews."

Lever: reframing. Separates you from every "builder". The brand's spine. → homepage headline.

6

Kill the stack

"You don't need 4 subscriptions. You need one."

Lever: simplicity + savings. Names the $60–100/mo tab of stitching Teal + Rezi + Jobscan. → comparison page.

7

Coffee-cold speed

"Five tailored applications before your coffee goes cold."

Lever: effort collapse. Sells the batch/Application-Kit magic. → demo video, Reels.

8

Jobs in your WhatsApp

"Job alerts in your WhatsApp — not your spam folder."

Lever: channel truth (MENA). A regionally obvious unlock nobody else offers. → Gulf landing, retention.

9

The tailoring truth

"Same CV to 50 jobs is why you hear nothing back."

Lever: pattern interrupt. Names the mistake, then sells per-job tailoring. → carousels, threads.

10

The contradiction catch

"Your CV and cover letter are contradicting each other. Recruiters notice."

Lever: insider secret. Justifies the "one source, consistent kit" feature. → LinkedIn, email.

04 — Content

Ten social posts, hooks first

Built for faceless short-form (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) and carousels — your AI-video pipeline's home turf. The hook is the first line on screen; it earns the next 2 seconds.

Post 01Reel · 15s
"3 words silently killing your CV."
Reveal them one by one on screen: "Responsible for" · "Worked on" · "Helped with." Show each replaced by a punchy metric bullet.
CTA: "Comment 'FIX' and I'll show you the swap." → drives DMs + comments.
Post 02Reel · before/after
"I ran my own CV through the tool. It was brutal."
Split-screen glow-up: weak bullets on the left, enhanced metric-rich bullets on the right, ATS score jumping 54 → 89.
CTA: "Try yours free — link in bio." Proof + product in one.
Post 03Screen-record · 20s
"This is what the ATS actually sees when it reads your CV."
Show the scan animation stripping formatting, missing keywords flashing red. Visceral "oh no" moment.
CTA: "Scan yours in 30 seconds." The fear hook in motion.
Post 04Carousel · 6 slides
"POV: you're an expat applying in Dubai — and your CV is missing THIS."
Slides walk through the Gulf-format fields (nationality, visa status, driving licence, photo) Western templates omit.
CTA: "Build a proper Gulf CV — link in bio." Owns the regional angle.
Post 05Reel · 60s demo
"Watch me turn one CV into a full job application in 60 seconds."
Real-time: upload → enhance → pick a job → generate kit → export. Timer ticking in the corner.
CTA: "The whole thing is free to try." Speed = the wow.
Post 06Talking-head / avatar
"Recruiters spend 6 seconds on your CV. Here's exactly where they look."
Overlay a heatmap on a sample CV — top third, job titles, dates. Explain what to put where.
CTA: "Get the layout right automatically." Educational + authority.
Post 07Reel · FR
"L'erreur de lettre de motivation que font 90% des candidats."
Show the generic "Je suis motivé…" opener vs a specific, employer-focused one. French audience, Le CYCLE cross-post.
CTA: "Génère la tienne en 30 secondes." Francophone wedge.
Post 08Carousel · 5 slides
"Same CV. 50 applications. 0 replies. Here's the fix."
Slide 1 the pain, slides 2–4 why tailoring beats spraying, slide 5 the one-click kit.
CTA: "Tailor every application — link in bio." Names the mistake, sells the cure.
Post 09Reel · listicle
"5 power verbs that make a recruiter stop scrolling."
Rapid cuts: Spearheaded · Drove · Cut · Scaled · Launched — each with a real bullet example.
CTA: "The tool rewrites your bullets this way." Saveable = shareable.
Post 10Reel · myth-bust
"Your cover letter and CV are contradicting each other — and recruiters notice."
Show two docs with mismatched dates/titles side by side, then the kit generating both from one profile.
CTA: "One source, zero contradictions." The consistency selling point.
Posting playbook Seed 8–10 of these two weeks before launch so there's a trail when people search you. Recycle each hook across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Pin the 60-second demo (Post 05). Every caption ends with the same bio CTA — consistency compounds.

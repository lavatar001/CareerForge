Frequently asked questions

Is my CV data private?

Yes. Your file is read in your own browser, stored in a private bucket only you can access, and you can delete everything at any time. Resumes are sensitive data — we treat them that way.

Will the AI invent experience I don’t have?

Never. The enhancer strengthens your own wording — it is explicitly forbidden from inventing metrics, employers or facts. Your original bullets are always kept.

Does the ATS score guarantee I pass?

No tool can promise that. Our score is an estimate built from keyword match, section completeness and formatting health — its real value is the missing-keywords list that tells you exactly what to fix.

Which countries and languages are supported?

Job feeds cover the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Egypt, France, the UK and the US. Documents can be generated in English, French or Arabic — including a Gulf CV format with visa status and nationality fields.
